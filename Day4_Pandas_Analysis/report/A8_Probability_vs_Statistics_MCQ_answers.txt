Assignment 8: Probability vs Statistics MCQ - Answers
====================================================

Source: Assignment 8 PROBABILITY vs STATISTICS.pdf

---

1. You know that a fair die has six equally likely outcomes. You want to calculate the probability of getting a 6. This is an example of:
A. Statistics
B. Probability
C. Regression
D. Sampling
Answer: B. Probability
Explanation: Using a known model (fair die) to predict outcome probability.

2. You roll a die 1,000 times and record the results. You notice that the number 6 appeared much more frequently than expected. You want to determine whether the die may be biased. This is primarily:
A. Probability
B. Statistics
C. Linear Algebra
D. Calculus
Answer: B. Statistics
Explanation: Using observed data to infer properties of the underlying process.

3. Which statement best describes Probability?
A. Using observed data to estimate unknown characteristics
B. Using a known model or assumptions to reason about possible outcomes
C. Organizing data into tables
D. Calculating averages from collected data
Answer: B. Using a known model or assumptions to reason about possible outcomes
Explanation: Probability = forward reasoning from model to outcomes.

4. Which statement best describes Statistics?
A. Predicting possible outcomes from a known probability model
B. Using observed data to understand or estimate an underlying process
C. Generating random numbers
D. Calculating only probabilities between 0 and 1
Answer: B. Using observed data to understand or estimate an underlying process
Explanation: Statistics = backward reasoning from data to model.

5. A company knows that historically 2% of its products are defective. It wants to estimate how many defective products might appear in the next 10,000 products.
This is primarily:
A. Statistics
B. Probability
C. Data cleaning
D. Visualization
Answer: B. Probability
Explanation: Using known defect rate (model) to predict future outcomes.

6. A company collects data from 10,000 products and finds that 250 are defective. It wants to estimate the defect rate of the production process.
This is primarily:
A. Probability
B. Statistics
C. Linear Algebra
D. Optimization
Answer: B. Statistics
Explanation: Using observed data (250/10000) to estimate underlying defect rate.

7. Which direction best represents Probability?
A. Data → Model
B. Model → Data
C. Data → Data
D. Model → Model
Answer: B. Model → Data
Explanation: Probability: known model → predict data/outcomes.

8. Which direction best represents Statistics?
A. Data → Model
B. Model → Data
C. Data → Data
D. Model → Model
Answer: A. Data → Model
Explanation: Statistics: observed data → infer model/parameters.

9. A weather forecast says there is a 70% chance of rain tomorrow. This statement comes from:
A. Statistics
B. Probability
C. Both equally
D. Neither
Answer: B. Probability
Explanation: Using meteorological models to predict future outcome probability.

10. A meteorologist analyzes 50 years of rainfall data and concludes that the average July rainfall is 150mm. This is:
A. Statistics
B. Probability
C. Both equally
D. Neither
Answer: A. Statistics
Explanation: Analyzing historical data to estimate a population parameter.

11. In a clinical trial, a new drug is tested on 1,000 patients. 850 recover. The estimated recovery rate is 85%. This is:
A. Statistics
B. Probability
C. Both equally
D. Neither
Answer: A. Statistics
Explanation: Using sample data (850/1000) to estimate population recovery rate.

12. Based on the 85% recovery rate from the clinical trial, a doctor tells a new patient: "You have an 85% chance of recovery." This statement uses:
A. Statistics
B. Probability
C. Both equally
D. Neither
Answer: B. Probability
Explanation: Using estimated rate (from statistics) to predict individual outcome probability.

13. A quality control engineer knows that a machine produces 5% defective items. She wants to calculate the probability that in a batch of 20 items, exactly 2 are defective. This uses:
A. Statistics
B. Probability
C. Both equally
D. Neither
Answer: B. Probability
Explanation: Using known defect rate (p=0.05) with binomial distribution to predict outcome.

14. The same engineer collects data from 50 batches and finds the actual defect rate is 7%. She updates her model. This is:
A. Statistics
B. Probability
C. Both equally
D. Neither
Answer: A. Statistics
Explanation: Using observed data to update the estimated parameter (from 5% to 7%).

15. A data scientist builds a model that predicts customer churn with 90% accuracy. The 90% accuracy is measured on:
A. Training data (Statistics)
B. Test data (Statistics)
C. Future predictions (Probability)
D. Model assumptions (Probability)
Answer: B. Test data (Statistics)
Explanation: Accuracy measured on observed test data is a statistical evaluation.

16. The same model is used to predict whether a specific customer will churn next month. The output "85% chance of churn" is:
A. Statistics
B. Probability
C. Both equally
D. Neither
Answer: B. Probability
Explanation: Applying the model to predict a specific future outcome.

17. The relationship between Probability and Statistics is:
A. They are completely separate fields
B. Statistics uses probability theory as its mathematical foundation
C. Probability uses statistical methods as its foundation
D. They are the same thing
Answer: B. Statistics uses probability theory as its mathematical foundation
Explanation: Statistical inference relies on probability distributions, sampling theory, etc.

18. Which of the following is a probabilistic statement?
A. "The average height in our sample is 170cm"
B. "There is a 95% chance the population mean is between 168cm and 172cm"
C. "The sample standard deviation is 10cm"
D. "We collected data from 100 people"
Answer: B. "There is a 95% chance the population mean is between 168cm and 172cm"
Explanation: Expresses uncertainty about a parameter using probability.

19. Which of the following is a statistical statement?
A. "If the coin is fair, the probability of 5 heads in a row is 3.125%"
B. "The probability of rain tomorrow is 30%"
C. "Based on our sample, we estimate the population mean is 50 with 95% CI [48, 52]"
D. "The theoretical variance of this distribution is 25"
Answer: C. "Based on our sample, we estimate the population mean is 50 with 95% CI [48, 52]"
Explanation: Inference from sample data to population parameter.

20. A casino knows that a slot machine has a 5% payout rate. A gambler plays 100 times. The casino's expected profit calculation uses:
A. Statistics
B. Probability
C. Both equally
D. Neither
Answer: B. Probability
Explanation: Using known payout rate (model) to compute expected outcomes.

21. The casino analyzes 1,000,000 plays and finds the actual payout rate is 4.8%. They adjust the machine. This is:
A. Statistics
B. Probability
C. Both equally
D. Neither
Answer: A. Statistics
Explanation: Using observed data to update the estimated payout rate.

22. In machine learning, training a model on labeled data is:
A. Statistics
B. Probability
C. Both equally
D. Neither
Answer: A. Statistics
Explanation: Learning patterns from observed data (statistical estimation).

22. Using the trained model to predict on new data is:
A. Statistics
B. Probability
C. Both equally
D. Neither
Answer: B. Probability
Explanation: Applying learned model to predict outcomes (probabilistic inference).

23. The fundamental difference:
A. Probability: Model → Data (deductive)
B. Statistics: Data → Model (inductive)
C. Both A and B
D. Neither
Answer: C. Both A and B
Explanation: Probability = forward/deductive (model→data). Statistics = backward/inductive (data→model).
