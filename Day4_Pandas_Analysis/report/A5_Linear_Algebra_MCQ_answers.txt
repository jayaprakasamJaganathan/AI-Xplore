PART A — VECTORS

1. Which of the following represents a vector?
A. 25
B. [2, 5, 7]
C. 2 + 5 + 7
D. x = 10
Answer: B. [2, 5, 7]
Explanation: A vector is an ordered list of numbers (array). 25 is a scalar, 2+5+7 is an expression, x=10 is an assignment.

2. If x = [2, 3] and y = [4, 5], what is x + y?
A. [6, 8]
B. [8, 15]
C. [2, 2]
D. [4, 3]
Answer: A. [6, 8]
Explanation: Vector addition is element-wise: [2+4, 3+5] = [6, 8].

3. If x = [2, 3], what is 4x?
A. [6, 7]
B. [8, 12]
C. [2, 12]
D. [4, 3]
Answer: B. [8, 12]
Explanation: Scalar multiplication: 4 × [2, 3] = [8, 12].

4. What does the magnitude of a vector represent?
A. Its direction
B. Its length
C. Its number of elements
D. Its number of dimensions only
Answer: B. Its length
Explanation: Magnitude (norm) is the length of the vector.

5. What is the Euclidean norm of the vector [3, 4]?
A. 3
B. 4
C. 5
D. 7
Answer: C. 5
Explanation: √(3² + 4²) = √(9+16) = √25 = 5.

6. Two vectors are orthogonal when:
A. They have the same magnitude
B. Their dot product is zero
C. Their values are identical
D. Their magnitudes are zero
Answer: B. Their dot product is zero
Explanation: Orthogonal vectors have dot product = 0.

7. What is the dot product of [1, 2] and [3, 4]?
A. 5
B. 7
C. 10
D. 11
Answer: D. 11
Explanation: 1×3 + 2×4 = 3 + 8 = 11.

8. If two non-zero vectors have a dot product of zero, they are:
A. Parallel
B. Orthogonal
Answer: B. Orthogonal
Explanation: Dot product = 0 means orthogonal (perpendicular).

PART B — MATRICES

9. What is the shape of a matrix with 3 rows and 4 columns?
A. (4, 3)
B. (3, 4)
C. (3, 3)
D. (4, 4)
Answer: B. (3, 4)
Explanation: Shape is (rows, columns).

10. If A is a 2×3 matrix and B is a 3×2 matrix, what is the shape of AB?
A. 2×2
B. 3×3
C. 2×3
D. 3×2
Answer: A. 2×2
Explanation: (2×3) × (3×2) = (2×2).

11. Matrix multiplication is:
A. Always commutative
B. Never commutative
C. Sometimes commutative
D. Only commutative for square matrices
Answer: B. Never commutative
Explanation: In general, AB ≠ BA.

12. What is the identity matrix?
A. A matrix with all zeros
B. A matrix with all ones
C. A square matrix with 1s on diagonal and 0s elsewhere
D. A matrix with random values
Answer: C. A square matrix with 1s on diagonal and 0s elsewhere

13. If A is a square matrix and A⁻¹ exists, then A × A⁻¹ = ?
A. 0
B. A
C. I (Identity matrix)
D. A²
Answer: C. I (Identity matrix)

14. The determinant of a 2×2 matrix [[a, b], [c, d]] is:
A. ad + bc
B. ad - bc
C. ab - cd
D. ac - bd
Answer: B. ad - bc

15. A matrix is invertible if and only if its determinant is:
A. Zero
B. Non-zero
C. Positive
D. Negative
Answer: B. Non-zero

16. The transpose of a matrix swaps:
A. Rows and columns
B. Diagonal elements
C. First and last row
D. Positive and negative values
Answer: A. Rows and columns

PART C — EIGENVALUES & EIGENVECTORS

17. For a square matrix A, if Av = λv for a non-zero vector v, then:
A. v is an eigenvector and λ is an eigenvalue
B. v is an eigenvalue and λ is an eigenvector
C. v is the inverse of A
D. λ is the determinant of A
Answer: A. v is an eigenvector and λ is an eigenvalue

18. The eigenvalues of a diagonal matrix are:
A. All zeros
B. The diagonal elements
C. All ones
D. The trace of the matrix
Answer: B. The diagonal elements

19. The trace of a matrix equals:
A. Sum of all elements
B. Product of diagonal elements
C. Sum of diagonal elements
D. Sum of eigenvalues
Answer: C. Sum of diagonal elements (also equals sum of eigenvalues)

20. If λ is an eigenvalue of A, then λ² is an eigenvalue of:
A. A²
B. A⁻¹
C. Aᵀ
D. 2A
Answer: A. A²

21. The rank of a matrix equals:
A. Number of non-zero eigenvalues
B. Number of rows
C. Number of columns
D. Determinant
Answer: A. Number of non-zero eigenvalues (for diagonalizable matrices)

PART D — LINEAR TRANSFORMATIONS

22. A linear transformation T satisfies:
A. T(u + v) = T(u) + T(v) and T(cv) = cT(v)
B. T(u + v) = T(u)T(v)
C. T(cv) = c + T(v)
D. T(u + v) = T(u) - T(v)
Answer: A. T(u + v) = T(u) + T(v) and T(cv) = cT(v)

23. The matrix representation of a linear transformation depends on:
A. The choice of basis
B. The determinant
C. The trace
D. The rank only
Answer: A. The choice of basis

23. A transformation is invertible if and only if its matrix is:
A. Symmetric
B. Invertible
C. Diagonal
D. Orthogonal
Answer: B. Invertible

24. The composition of two linear transformations corresponds to:
A. Matrix addition
B. Matrix multiplication
C. Element-wise multiplication
D. Matrix transpose
Answer: B. Matrix multiplication

25. A projection matrix P satisfies:
A. P² = P
B. P² = I
C. P² = 0
D. P² = 2P
Answer: A. P² = P
