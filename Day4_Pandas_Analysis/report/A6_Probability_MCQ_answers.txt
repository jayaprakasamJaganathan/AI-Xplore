PART A — BASIC PROBABILITY

1. The probability of an event can have a value between:
A. -1 and 1
B. 0 and 1
C. 1 and 100
D. -∞ and +∞
Answer: B. 0 and 1
Explanation: Probability is always between 0 (impossible) and 1 (certain).

2. If the probability of an event is 0, the event is:
A. Certain
B. Likely
C. Impossible
D. Equally likely
Answer: C. Impossible
Explanation: Probability 0 means the event cannot occur.

3. If the probability of an event is 1, the event is:
A. Impossible
B. Certain
C. Unlikely
D. Random
Answer: B. Certain
Explanation: Probability 1 means the event will definitely occur.

4. A fair coin is tossed once. What is the probability of getting Heads?
A. 0
B. 0.25
C. 0.5
D. 1
Answer: C. 0.5
Explanation: Fair coin has two equally likely outcomes.

5. A fair six-sided die is rolled once. What is the probability of getting a 4?
A. 1/2
B. 1/4
C. 1/6
D. 4/6
Answer: C. 1/6
Explanation: One favorable outcome out of 6 equally likely outcomes.

6. A fair die is rolled once. What is the probability of getting an even number?
A. 1/6
B. 1/3
C. 1/2
D. 2/3
Answer: C. 1/2
Explanation: Even numbers are 2, 4, 6 (3 out of 6 outcomes = 1/2).

7. If P(A) = 0.3, what is P(not A)?
A. 0.3
B. 0.5
C. 0.7
D. 1.3
Answer: C. 0.7
Explanation: P(not A) = 1 - P(A) = 1 - 0.3 = 0.7.

8. Which of the following represents the complement of event A?
A. A + B
B. A ∩ B
C. A'
D. A ∪ B
Answer: C. A'
Explanation: Complement of A is denoted A' or Aᶜ.

PART B — CONDITIONAL PROBABILITY

9. P(A|B) represents:
A. Probability of A and B
B. Probability of A given B
C. Probability of B given A
D. Probability of A or B
Answer: B. Probability of A given B
Explanation: P(A|B) = P(A and B) / P(B).

10. If P(A) = 0.4, P(B) = 0.5, and P(A and B) = 0.2, what is P(A|B)?
A. 0.2
B. 0.4
C. 0.5
D. 0.8
Answer: B. 0.4
Explanation: P(A|B) = P(A and B) / P(B) = 0.2 / 0.5 = 0.4.

11. If A and B are independent, then P(A|B) = ?
A. P(A)
B. P(B)
C. P(A and B)
D. 0
Answer: A. P(A)
Explanation: For independent events, P(A|B) = P(A).

12. Bayes' theorem states:
A. P(A|B) = P(B|A) × P(A) / P(B)
B. P(A|B) = P(A) × P(B) / P(B|A)
C. P(A|B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B)
D. P(A|B) = P(A) × P(B)
Answer: A. P(A|B) = P(B|A) × P(A) / P(B)

13. A medical test has 95% sensitivity and 90% specificity. Disease prevalence is 1%. If test is positive, what is P(Disease|Positive)?
A. 95%
B. ~8.8%
C. 90%
D. 1%
Answer: B. ~8.8%
Explanation: P(D|+) = (0.95 × 0.01) / (0.95×0.01 + 0.10×0.99) ≈ 0.0876 ≈ 8.8%.

PART C — RANDOM VARIABLES & DISTRIBUTIONS

14. A random variable is:
A. A variable that changes randomly
B. A function that assigns a numerical value to each outcome
C. A variable that cannot be predicted
D. A variable with no pattern
Answer: B. A function that assigns a numerical value to each outcome

15. The expected value of a discrete random variable X is:
A. The most likely value
B. The average value over many trials
C. The maximum possible value
D. The median value
Answer: B. The average value over many trials
Explanation: E[X] = Σ x × P(X=x).

16. For a binomial distribution with n=10, p=0.3, the expected value is:
A. 3
B. 3.3
C. 2.1
D. 0.3
Answer: A. 3
Explanation: E[X] = n × p = 10 × 0.3 = 3.

17. The variance of a binomial distribution is:
A. np
B. np(1-p)
C. np²
D. p(1-p)
Answer: B. np(1-p)

18. A normal distribution is characterized by:
A. Mean and median
B. Mean and standard deviation
C. Median and mode
D. Range and IQR
Answer: B. Mean and standard deviation

19. In a standard normal distribution, approximately what percentage of data falls within 1 standard deviation of the mean?
A. 68%
B. 95%
C. 99.7%
D. 50%
Answer: A. 68%

20. The Central Limit Theorem states that:
A. Sample means are always normally distributed
B. The sampling distribution of the sample mean approaches normal as sample size increases
C. All distributions are normal
D. Sample size doesn't matter
Answer: B. The sampling distribution of the sample mean approaches normal as sample size increases

PART D — PROBABILITY RULES

21. P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B) is the:
A. Addition rule
B. Multiplication rule
C. Complement rule
D. Bayes' rule
Answer: A. Addition rule

22. If A and B are mutually exclusive, then P(A ∩ B) = ?
A. P(A) × P(B)
B. P(A) + P(B)
C. 0
D. 1
Answer: C. 0

23. If A and B are independent, then P(A ∩ B) = ?
A. P(A) + P(B)
B. P(A) × P(B)
C. P(A|B)
D. 0
Answer: B. P(A) × P(B)

24. The Law of Total Probability states:
A. P(A) = Σ P(A|Bᵢ)P(Bᵢ) for partition Bᵢ
B. P(A) = P(A|B) + P(A|B')
C. P(A) = P(A and B) + P(A and B')
D. P(A) = P(A|B)P(B)
Answer: A. P(A) = Σ P(A|Bᵢ)P(Bᵢ) for partition Bᵢ

25. If P(A) = 0.6, P(B) = 0.7, and A and B are independent, what is P(A ∪ B)?
A. 1.3
B. 0.88
C. 0.42
D. 0.13
Answer: B. 0.88
Explanation: P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A)P(B) = 0.6 + 0.7 - 0.42 = 0.88.
